Frequently Asked Questions

Does microneedling hurt?

With our pre-treatment topical numbing cream, most clients describe the feeling as a mild vibration or sandpaper texture rather than pain. Areas over bone (such as the forehead) may feel slightly more sensitive, but the session remains very tolerable.

What is the downtime after microneedling?

You can expect mild redness similar to a moderate sunburn for 24 to 48 hours. Most clients resume daily routines the following morning. We provide clear post-care instructions and recommend avoiding strenuous workouts, direct sun, and makeup for 24 hours.

How many sessions will I need?

While visible improvements in skin radiance and firmness appear within 2 weeks of the first session, optimal collagen remodeling for acne scarring or deep texture occurs over a course of 3 to 6 sessions spaced 4 weeks apart.
