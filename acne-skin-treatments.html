Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Green Peel® herbal treatment?

Dr. med. Christine Schrammek’s Green Peel is a world-renowned, purely biological herbal peeling method. Depending on skin severity, we offer both the gentle "Fresh Up" / "Energy" revitalizing protocols and the intensive "Classic" peeling that induces natural epidermal peeling over 5 days, revealing completely renewed skin.

How quickly will I see improvement in my acne?

Active inflammation and redness typically subside noticeably within 48 to 72 hours of your first targeted clarifying session. For severe or hormonal acne, steady pore clearing and sebum balance is usually achieved through an initial program of 3 to 6 treatments spaced 2 to 4 weeks apart.

Can you treat post-acne dark marks and scarring?

Yes. Once active inflammatory acne is stabilized, we transition your protocol toward microneedling collagen induction therapy or diamond microdermabrasion to smooth textural atrophic scarring and fade stubborn red and brown marks (PIE and PIH).
