Frequently Asked Questions

What does TempSure Envi skin tightening feel like?

Clients describe the sensation as a soothing, gliding warm massage. A specialized conductive gel is applied and the smooth handpiece is moved in gentle circular motions. There is no sharp pain, snapping, or burning sensation.

Is there any downtime after the session?

Zero downtime. You may notice a faint, healthy rosy flush immediately following the procedure that typically subsides within an hour. You can immediately return to work, apply makeup, and continue your day.

How many sessions are recommended for optimal firmness?

We typically recommend a series of 3 to 5 treatments spaced 3 to 4 weeks apart. Because collagen synthesis takes time, maximum skin density and contour definition reveal themselves over 2 to 3 months following your series.
