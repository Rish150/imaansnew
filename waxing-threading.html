Frequently Asked Questions

Should I choose threading or waxing for my eyebrows?

Threading is the gold standard for eyebrows and facial hair because it allows hair-by-hair precision without pulling on the delicate skin around the orbital eye area. Threading is also completely safe for clients using retinol, tretinoin, Accutane, or chemical peels where waxing is contraindicated. Waxing is ideal for larger body areas such as legs, underarms, and bikini.

How long should my hair be before a waxing appointment?

For body waxing (legs, arms, bikini), hair should ideally be approximately 1/4 inch long (roughly the length of a grain of rice) — usually 2 to 3 weeks of natural growth after shaving. Threading can grip much shorter, finer hairs.

How do I prevent ingrown hairs and bumps after waxing?

We recommend gentle exfoliation 48 hours after your session using our Phytoderm Peel Toner or a soft washcloth, wearing loose cotton clothing, and avoiding saunas, steam rooms, or strenuous exercise for 24 hours.
