Laser Hair Removal in London, Ontario

The Vectus diode laser at Imaan's Studio clears a full leg in under thirty minutes, uses contact cooling to reduce discomfort, and is calibrated per-client with a Skintel melanin reading — so it works safely across skin tones, not just the easiest cases.

Why Vectus Over IPL

  • Contact-cooling tip reduces discomfort during treatment
  • Largest spot size on the market — fewer passes per session
  • Skintel melanin reading calibrates energy to your exact skin tone
  • Used by medical professionals, not a consumer-grade IPL device

Frequently Asked Questions

How many sessions will I need?

Your technician determines a session count at consultation based on hair density, area and skin tone — most clients see meaningful reduction within a course of sessions rather than one visit.

Does it hurt?

The Vectus laser's contact-cooling tip is designed specifically to reduce the discomfort associated with older laser and IPL technology.

Is it safe for darker skin tones?

Yes — the Skintel melanin tester reads your skin before treatment so the laser's energy setting is matched to you, rather than using one setting for everyone.
